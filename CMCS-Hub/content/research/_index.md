## 1. Cardiovascular Disease Risk Factor Discovery &amp; Risk Assessment

**1992&mdash;2024**

### 1.1 Primordial Prevention: Discovery of Novel Cardiovascular Risk Factors

Our work focuses on identifying the "risk factors of risk factors"&mdash;novel lipid-related biomarkers and metabolic signatures that emerge before the onset of established cardiovascular risk factors such as hypertension and diabetes. Using deeply phenotyped Chinese cohorts, we have discovered previously unrecognized lipoprotein subspecies associated with subclinical atherosclerosis, providing new targets for early intervention before clinical disease manifests.

### 1.2 Primary Prevention: Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Tool

We developed and validated a cardiovascular disease risk prediction model specifically calibrated for Chinese populations. This model integrates population-specific risk factor distributions, ethnic-specific biomarker thresholds, and longitudinal cohort data to accurately estimate individual CVD risk. The risk assessment tool was incorporated into the [**Chinese guideline on the primary prevention of cardiovascular diseases**](https://mednexus.org/doi/full/10.1097/CD9.0000000000000025#tab-citations) and has been adopted by national clinical practice guidelines, reshaping primary prevention strategies across the country.

### 1.3 Secondary Prevention: Healthcare Quality Evaluation

We evaluate healthcare quality and clinical outcomes for patients with established cardiovascular disease, examining prognostic factors that drive recurrent events, heart failure progression, and mortality. This work informs risk-stratified follow-up protocols and personalized secondary prevention strategies, aiming to close the gap between guideline recommendations and real-world clinical practice.

## 2. CMCS-xOmics Project

**2024&ndash;Present**

### 2.1 Novel Methods for Multi-Omics Integrative Analysis

We develop new analytical frameworks for integrating multi-omics data across the genome-proteome-metabolome-phenome continuum. Our methodological innovations include network-based mediation analysis to trace molecular chains from genetic variants to disease phenotypes, Bayesian hierarchical models for cross-platform data harmonization, Mendelian randomization leveraging multi-omics instrumental variables, and deep learning architectures for multi-modal omics data fusion.

### 2.2 Drug Target Discovery &amp; Translational Research

We apply our multi-omics integration framework to systematically identify, validate, and translate novel therapeutic targets for cardiovascular disease. Our pipeline combines Mendelian randomization, colocalization analysis, and proteome-wide association studies to prioritize candidate targets, followed by in vitro and in vivo functional validation and preclinical development with biomarker-guided patient stratification for clinical trials.

### 2.3 Aging Multi-Omics &amp; Precision Risk Assessment

We characterize proteomic and metabolomic trajectories across the adult lifespan in Chinese populations, mapping the molecular signatures that distinguish healthy aging from accelerated biological aging. By integrating these aging-related profiles with cardiovascular outcomes, we develop multi-omics-informed risk prediction models that incorporate polygenic risk scores, proteomic signatures, and metabolomic profiles to enable individualized risk stratification and early intervention.
