We integrate population-based cohorts, multi-omics data (genomics, proteomics, metabolomics), and advanced analytical methods to decode the molecular mechanisms of cardiovascular disease. Our mission is to discover novel risk factors, identify drug targets, and build precision risk assessment tools for Chinese populations.
