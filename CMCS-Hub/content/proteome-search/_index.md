---
title: "Protein Lookup"
description: "Search the results of the 20-year CMCS proteomic analysis by UniProt ID, gene symbol, Ensembl gene ID, or SOMAmer ID."
---
This lookup exposes the protein-level results underlying *A 20-year longitudinal proteomic analysis prioritizes drug target proteins for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease*. Each entry collects everything reported for one protein in the supplementary tables: discovery associations, GSMR2 Mendelian randomization (whole-blood and cis), gene-based tests, and replication.

Enter **any one** of the following identifiers and press Enter:

- **UniProt ID** — e.g. `Q9NQ94`
- **Gene symbol** — e.g. `A1CF`, `ABO`
- **Ensembl gene ID** — e.g. `ENSG00000148584`
- **SOMAmer ID** — e.g. `seq.12423.38`

Several identifiers can be queried at once by separating them with spaces or commas. Results are shown as one card per protein; each card can be downloaded as a TSV file or shared with the **Copy link** button.
