## The CMCS Cohort

Established in 1992, the Chinese Multi-Provincial Cohort Study (CMCS) recruited over 30,000 healthy adults aged ≥35 years from 16 centers across 11 provinces and municipalities nationwide. Baseline examination included comprehensive questionnaires, physical examinations, and laboratory tests. Among them, 21,953 participants have been followed for over 30 years, with hard endpoints including incident cardiovascular events and all-cause mortality collected. Large-scale repeat surveys were conducted in Beijing (2002, 2007, 2012, 2020-2023) and outside Beijing (2007), providing a uniquely rich longitudinal resource for cardiovascular research.

## The CMCS-xOmics Project

The CMCS-xOmics project extends the CMCS cohort with deep multi-omics profiling. In 2021, over 3,000 Beijing participants underwent ASA ChIP genotyping (~730k markers). In 2023, over 3,000 participants from regions outside Beijing received whole-genome sequencing (DNASEQ T7, ~30× coverage). In 2024, longitudinal proteomics (Soma 11K panel) and metabolomics (UHPLC-Q-TOF MS, pseudo-targeted) profiling was initiated for Beijing participants with available blood samples. Earlier efforts include NMR lipidomics profiling of blood samples from 2002. Vascular phenotyping includes carotid ultrasonography at the 2002, 2007, and 2012 surveys, and retinal vascular imaging plus arterial elasticity assessment at the 2012 and 2020–2023 surveys — providing comprehensive genotype-phenotype data for multi-omics cardiovascular research.
