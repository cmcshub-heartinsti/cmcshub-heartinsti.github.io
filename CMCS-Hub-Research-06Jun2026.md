# CMCS-Hub Research: Cardiovascular Disease

**Version**: June 6, 2026

---

## 1. Cardiovascular Disease Risk Factor Discovery & Risk Assessment (1992—2024)

### 1.1 Primordial Prevention: Discovery of Novel Cardiovascular Risk Factors
Focuses on identifying the "risk factors of risk factors"&mdash;novel lipid-related biomarkers and metabolic signatures emerging before established CVD risk factors. Using deeply phenotyped Chinese cohorts, we have discovered previously unrecognized lipoprotein subspecies associated with subclinical atherosclerosis.

### 1.2 Primary Prevention: "China Solution" for Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
A CVD risk prediction model specifically calibrated for Chinese populations, integrating population-specific risk factor distributions and ethnic-specific biomarker thresholds. Adopted by national clinical practice guidelines, reshaping primary prevention strategies across China.

### 1.3 Secondary Prevention: Healthcare Quality Evaluation
Evaluates healthcare quality and clinical outcomes for patients with established CVD, examining prognostic factors driving recurrent events, heart failure progression, and mortality. Informs risk-stratified follow-up protocols and personalized secondary prevention.

## 2. CMCS-xOmics Project (2024—Present)

### 2.1 Novel Methods for Multi-Omics Integrative Analysis
Develops analytical frameworks integrating multi-omics data across the genome-proteome-metabolome-phenome continuum. Innovations include network-based mediation analysis, Bayesian hierarchical models for cross-platform harmonization, Mendelian randomization with multi-omics instrumental variables, and deep learning architectures for multi-modal omics fusion.

### 2.2 Drug Target Discovery & Translational Research
Systematically identifies, validates, and translates novel therapeutic targets using multi-omics-informed prioritization (MR, colocalization, PWAS), followed by in vitro and in vivo functional validation, preclinical development, and biomarker-guided patient stratification.

### 2.3 Aging Multi-Omics & Precision Risk Assessment
Characterizes proteomic and metabolomic trajectories across the adult lifespan, mapping molecular signatures that distinguish healthy from accelerated aging. Develops multi-omics-informed CVD risk prediction models integrating polygenic risk scores, proteomic signatures, and metabolomic profiles.
